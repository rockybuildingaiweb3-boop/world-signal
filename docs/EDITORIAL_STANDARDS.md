# Editorial Standards

WORLD SIGNAL is not a headline aggregator. Selection, verification, context, and analysis matter more than volume.

## Selection threshold

Include a story when it materially affects international security, diplomacy, political stability, policy, humanitarian conditions, public safety, climate, AI research or capability, macroeconomics, financial stability, labour, trade, or structural economic change.

Exclude routine corporate announcements, minor product updates, ordinary market fluctuations, and viral posts unless there is a material consequence. There is no quota per domain. A quiet news day should remain quiet.

## Story hierarchy

The Executive Briefing selects three to five top signals across all domains. Rank by consequence, novelty, evidence quality, and relevance—not dramatic wording.

Importance:
- 5 — systemic, historic, or broad global consequences
- 4 — major national, regional, sectoral, or technological consequences
- 3 — material but bounded development
- 2 — limited, mainly worth monitoring
- 1 — normally exclude

Importance is not confidence. An event can be highly important while attribution remains uncertain.

## Analytical separation

For every signal, distinguish:
- What happened
- Verified facts
- Source-reported information
- Official or company claims
- Analysis
- Uncertainty
- Conditional scenarios
- Observable watchpoints

Never write analysis as if it were an independently reported fact.

## World Events

For conflict and politically sensitive events, distinguish occurrence, attribution, target identification, casualty figures, claims of responsibility, official denials, and independent verification. Preserve competing accounts and state the evidence supporting each.

## AI Frontier

Record the model/system name and version, research group, release date, paper/benchmark/model card/code/technical report, evaluation method, comparison baseline, and whether results are self-reported, peer-reviewed, independently tested, or independently reproduced.

A benchmark score is not general intelligence. A demo is not a deployed capability. A preprint is not peer review. A company announcement is not independent verification.

## Economic Outlook

Keep macroeconomic signals separate from market price action. For material data, state measurement period, release date, units, revisions, seasonal adjustment, and official publisher.

Where evidence permits, discuss near-term (1–3 months), medium-term (6–18 months), and structural (3–5 years) implications. These are analytical horizons, not guarantees.

### Personal relevance

Translate signals into general decision support for career/skills, labour-market exposure, household cash flow, emergency resilience, borrowing, major spending, purchasing power, and risk awareness.

Do not recommend securities, trades, leverage, or portfolio allocations. Do not imply knowledge of an individual reader's complete finances.

## Style

Be concise, precise, and neutral. Use absolute dates. Preserve original names, paper titles, model versions, units, and official terminology. Avoid unsupported superlatives. Prefer evidence-qualified phrasing.

## Corrections

Do not silently erase meaningful errors. State the affected signal, earlier claim, correction, reason, supporting source, and correction time. Record corrections in the current edition's audit section. Git history retains earlier versions.
