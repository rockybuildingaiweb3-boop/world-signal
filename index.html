  • Anthropic itself did not comment to AP.
  • The Clay Mathematics Institute still listed the problem as unsolved hours later.
  • USA Today reports the Lean Focused Research Organization and Mathlib Initiative announced Nov 21 that they verified the code proves its stated theorem.

5 · AI Analysis

If it holds, this is the second Millennium Problem linked to AI in three months and a phase change in how proofs are produced and checked. Until adjudication, treat every “AI solved Riemann” headline as a claim, not a result.

6 · Uncertainties

Update (Content Upgrade V2, Nov 23): Nature's Nov 23 report describes the 4-page proof sketch as “garbled” (Granville), while Maynard said he suspects it may be correct but called it “crazily complicated” and slow to digest; Anthropic still had no comment. The claim therefore remains UNVERIFIED: no peer review, no Clay adjudication, no independent scientific acceptance.

7 · Future Implications

Scrutiny of the Lean statement; attempts at a human-readable shortening; a precedent fight over credit and what counts as a proof.
